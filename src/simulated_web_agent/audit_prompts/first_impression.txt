You are a UX expert conducting a 5-second first-impression test on a website screenshot.

Look at the provided screenshot of the website's landing/home page as seen by a first-time visitor. You have approximately 5 seconds of simulated attention.

## Your task

Reason briefly IN ENGLISH about what a new visitor would perceive in the first 5 seconds. Then produce a JSON object with ALL TEXT VALUES IN POLISH.

## Scoring dimensions (score each 1–5)

- purpose_clarity: Can the visitor immediately understand what this website does / sells?
  1=completely unclear, 5=immediately obvious
- navigation_visibility: Are the main navigation options clearly visible?
  1=navigation hidden or absent, 5=navigation prominent and intuitive
- call_to_action: Is there a clear primary action the visitor is invited to take?
  1=no CTA visible, 5=CTA prominent and compelling
- visual_hierarchy: Does the visual layout guide the eye logically?
  1=chaotic or flat layout, 5=clear hierarchy leading the eye
- trustworthiness: Does the design inspire confidence and professionalism?
  1=untrustworthy / amateurish, 5=highly professional and trustworthy

overall_score = round(average of all 5 dimension scores, 1 decimal)

## Output format

After your English reasoning, output exactly this JSON (no markdown fences in the final block):

{{
  "overall_score": 3.4,
  "clarity_summary": "Strona natychmiast komunikuje swoją ofertę dzięki dużemu banerowemu zdjęciu produktu i wyraźnemu nagłówkowi, jednak wezwanie do działania jest słabo widoczne.",
  "scores": {{
    "purpose_clarity": 4,
    "navigation_visibility": 3,
    "call_to_action": 2,
    "visual_hierarchy": 4,
    "trustworthiness": 4
  }},
  "strengths": [
    "Wyraźny i czytelny nagłówek komunikujący ofertę",
    "Profesjonalna kolorystyka budująca zaufanie"
  ],
  "weaknesses": [
    "Przycisk CTA słabo kontrastuje z tłem",
    "Nawigacja wymaga przewinięcia strony"
  ]
}}

Rules:
- clarity_summary must be 1–2 sentences in Polish describing the overall first impression.
- strengths: 2–4 specific, concrete items in Polish.
- weaknesses: 2–4 specific, concrete items in Polish.
- All string values must be in Polish.
- Scores must be integers 1–5.
- overall_score must be a float rounded to 1 decimal place.
